FAQs

  1. What services do warehouse logistics companies provide?
    They handle storage, inventory tracking, fulfillment, courier coordination, and distribution.
  2. How to choose a warehouse logistics provider in the Philippines?
    Evaluate storage quality, fulfillment speed, technology integration, customer support, and pricing transparency.
  3. What is the difference between warehousing and 3PL logistics?
    Warehousing focuses on storage, while 3PL covers storage, fulfillment, dispatch, and returns management.
